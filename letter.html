Dear Fatima 🌷

✿

Hey Potato,

Happy Birthday. 🤍

I don't think I've ever properly told you how much your friendship means to me.

Somewhere along the way, you stopped being just a friend and became someone who feels more like family. Like the annoying little sister I somehow ended up with.

We've had our random conversations, our stupid jokes, our disagreements, and all those completely ordinary moments that somehow became memories.

And honestly, I wouldn't trade those moments for anything.

I know things haven't always gone perfectly. I've made mistakes, and there are things I wish I had handled differently.

But regardless of everything that has happened, I genuinely hope you know that I value you, I respect you, and I'm grateful that our paths crossed.

Today isn't about any of that, though. Today is about you.

I hope this new year of your life brings you peaceful days, genuine happiness, good people, countless reasons to smile, and everything you've been quietly wishing for.

Keep being the weird, funny, kind, occasionally annoying Potato that you are. 🥔

And please remember that no matter how much time passes, you'll always have a special place in my life.

Happy Birthday, Fatima. 🌷

Stay happy. Stay you.

— From someone who is very glad to call you family 🤍
